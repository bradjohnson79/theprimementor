# Search intent

Search intent is not the same as topic similarity.

`what is a natal chart` and `buy detailed natal chart report` share a topic and do not share commercial intent.

PMA classifies:

- Transactional
- Commercial investigation
- Problem / desire
- Informational
- Poor fit

Do not buy informational traffic just because it mentions astrology.

Do not invent search volume, CPC, or competition. If those numbers are missing, they are unknown.
