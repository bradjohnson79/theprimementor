# Playbook: clicks but no conversions

Diagnosis: ads attract clicks; purchases do not follow.

Evidence to inspect: CTR if visible, Umani /reports bounce, CTA clicks, checkout starts, purchase events.

Interpretation: the breakdown is often after the click. Check intent/landing congruence before rewriting an engaging ad.

Recommendation: keep the ad, test the landing message or the query match.

Confidence stays low until purchase sample size is meaningful.
